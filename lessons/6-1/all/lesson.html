📁 Case file: ultra-fast fashion apps

May 2025The European Commission and national consumer authorities told Shein to stop fake discounts and pressure selling, for example false purchase deadlines.
June 2025European consumer groups complained about Shein’s dark patterns: countdown timers, ‘low stock’ messages that may not be true and endless scroll.
July 2025France fined Shein €40 million for misleading discounts and unproven environmental claims.
2026The EU fined Temu €200 million under the Digital Services Act: it did not properly assess the risk of illegal products on its marketplace.
2024A Swiss NGO, Public Eye, reported that workers at some Shein suppliers still worked around 75 hours a week.

PROJECT: Survey — How ethical are your clothes and apps?

9AYou are going to survey your classmates or colleagues. Prepare questions before you start. You want to find out:

1who owns a garment made from organic or fair-trade cotton
2who sometimes wears vintage or second-hand clothes
3who often gives clothes away to charity
4who has bought clothes made of recycled materials
5who wears leather goods
6how often people buy fast fashion items (e.g. every two months / once a year)
7where your colleagues’ clothes were made
8who has bought clothes in an app like Shein or Temu apps
9who has bought something because of a countdown timer or a ‘low stock’ message apps
10who has deleted a shopping app because it was too addictive apps
11who has returned or thrown away an item after wearing it fewer than five times apps

💡 Use different question types: yes/no (Have you ever…?), multiple choice (How often…? a) b) c)), open (Why…?). Start general, then ask more personal questions.
